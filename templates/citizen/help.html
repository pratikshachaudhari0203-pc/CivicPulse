Learn how to use CivicPulse and manage your complaints.

How CivicPulse Works

1

Report an Issue

Select a category, describe the civic problem, provide the location and submit your complaint.

2

Track Your Complaint

Open My Complaints and select View Details to check the current status of your complaint.

3

Issue Gets Resolved

The concerned officer handles the issue and updates the complaint status.

Complaint Status

Pending

Your complaint has been submitted and is waiting to be reviewed.

In Progress

Your complaint is currently being handled.

Resolved

The reported issue has been resolved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a complaint?

Go to Report an Issue, select the appropriate category, enter the issue details and location, then submit the form.

Where can I see my complaints?

Open My Complaints from the sidebar to view all complaints submitted from your account.

How can I track my complaint?

Open My Complaints and click View Details on a complaint to see its current progress.

What information should I provide?

Provide a clear description and accurate location of the civic issue. You can also attach an image if available.
